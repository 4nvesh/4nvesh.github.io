Digital paintings

Learning to see before learning to render.

Digital painting began as an exercise in observation—studying faces, value, colour and the way light changes a story. It became a counterweight to engineering: slower, more intuitive and comfortable with ambiguity.

I move between portrait studies and cinematic scenes, using each painting to make the next one a little more intentional.
