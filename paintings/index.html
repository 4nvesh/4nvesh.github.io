Digital paintings / an evolving practice

Painting is how I learn to notice.

Light before colour. Shape before detail. Feeling before polish.

The journey so far

Observation became another engineering tool.

I began digital painting by trying to reproduce what I saw. Portrait studies in Clip Studio Paint and Photoshop taught me that a convincing image is less about adding detail and more about understanding proportion, value, edges and light.

Over time, the work became more narrative. I started asking what the light was doing emotionally, what could stay hidden and how colour could carry the scene. That sensitivity now returns to my product work—in hierarchy, material, form and the way an interface directs attention.

This gallery is intentionally alive. New paintings will join it as the practice grows.

Different medium. Same curiosity.
