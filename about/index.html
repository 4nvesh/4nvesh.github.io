About Anvesh

I never really chose between engineering and art.

That tension used to feel like indecision. It became the thing that lets me move comfortably between mechanisms, interfaces and stories.

The original 2022 room survives here as an image—same atmosphere, no 3D engine.
Nyctophile

Someone who finds comfort and creative energy in the night.

Curiosity is the thread.

I completed a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, where I learned to think in systems, communicate technical ideas and develop computer-aided drawings and designs.

Outside that path, I kept learning: CS50 introduced me to computer science; web development gave me a way to build interfaces; digital painting sharpened how I see light, proportion and composition.

Today, those interests no longer compete. They meet in product work that needs engineering discipline, visual judgement and a clear digital experience.
